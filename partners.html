What Partnership Looks Like

Personal Evaluations

An individual health assessment for every resident, not a one-size-fits-all checklist.

Behavioral Health Built In

Addiction treatment and counseling coordinated with primary care.

Medication Reconciliation

One complete, accurate medication list per resident, built with the brown bag method.

Referrals Handled

We manage referrals, insurance authorization, scheduling and visit preparation.

Remote Collaboration

We stay in touch with your staff between in-person visits.

Regular Presence

Patients seen three to five days a week, so care stays consistent.

How It Works

  1. 1. Intro Call

    Tell us about your program, your residents and the care gaps you're seeing.

  2. 2. Care Plan

    We agree on a visit schedule and how we'll work alongside your staff.

  3. 3. Ongoing Care

    Residents get evaluations, follow-up and referrals, with regular check-ins with your team.
