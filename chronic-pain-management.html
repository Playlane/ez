About This Service

Living with ongoing pain affects sleep, mood, work and relationships. We take time to understand your pain, what makes it better or worse, and how it affects your life.

Together we build a plan that may include a medication review, lifestyle changes, and referrals to specialists or therapy when they would help.

What to Expect

  • A full assessment of your pain and its history
  • A review of current and past treatments
  • A plan built around your goals
  • Referrals to specialists when helpful

What to Bring

  • Your insurance card
  • Every medication you take, in one bag
  • Notes on when your pain is better or worse
