About This Service

If you've been dealing with changes in mood, anxiety, sleep, memory or behavior, a psychiatric evaluation is a first step toward understanding what's happening.

We listen, ask questions about your history and daily life, and then talk with you about a plan. That may include treatment here, medication review, counseling or a referral.

Book your free consultation

What to Expect

  • A private, judgment-free conversation
  • Questions about your history, symptoms and daily life
  • A clear explanation of what we find
  • A plan for next steps

What to Bring

  • Your insurance card
  • Every medication you take, in one bag
  • Notes about what you've been experiencing
