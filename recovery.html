Treatment in a Regular Doctor's Office

Office-based addiction treatment (OBAT) means you get care for substance use disorder in the same kind of setting where you'd get a checkup. No separate clinic, no starting over with strangers.

Because the same team handles your primary care, we see the whole picture: your physical health, your medications and your recovery goals.

What Recovery Care Includes

Medical Treatment

Office-based treatment for substance use disorder, managed by your primary care team.

Counseling

One-on-one sessions to understand triggers, build coping skills and plan for the long term.

Medication Review

We check every medicine you take so your treatment and other prescriptions work safely together.

The Same Provider

A provider who knows your history and keeps showing up for you, visit after visit.

FAQ

Will anyone know I'm getting addiction treatment?

Your visits happen in a regular primary care office, and your health information is kept private.

Do I have to be ready to stop completely?

No. Come as you are. The free consultation is a place to talk honestly about where you are and what you want.

What if I need more support than you provide?

We'll help you find it. Our referral support covers the referral, insurance authorization and scheduling.
