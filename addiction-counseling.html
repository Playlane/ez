About This Service

Recovery takes more than medicine. Counseling gives you a space to understand your triggers, work through stress and build habits that support the life you want.

Your counseling works alongside your medical care, so your treatment and your counseling move in the same direction.

Book your free consultation

What to Expect

  • Regular one-on-one sessions at a pace that fits you
  • Practical tools for cravings, stress and setbacks
  • Goals you set together and revisit over time
  • Coordination with your medical care

What to Bring

  • An open mind. You don't need to have everything figured out.
  • Anything you'd like to talk about first
