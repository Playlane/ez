Dr. Florence Eze, DNP, NP

Dr. Eze is a gerontology nurse practitioner and Doctor of Nursing Practice with over nine years of experience caring for adult and geriatric patients.

She leads care at EZ Health, bringing primary care and behavioral health together so that patients can be seen as whole people, with one provider who understands their history, their medications and their goals.
