FAQ

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. Your first conversation with us is free, so you can ask questions and decide whether we're the right fit before booking a visit.

Do you take my insurance?

We accept Aetna, Humana, Medicaid of Maryland, Medicare Part B and UnitedHealthcare. If your plan isn't listed, call us at 227-259-9679 and we'll check your coverage with you.

Can I get primary care and addiction treatment in the same place?

Yes. Your primary care and behavioral health care are coordinated by the same team in the same office.

What should I bring to my first visit?

A photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and every medication you currently take, in one bag.
